Our Story

Made by hand,
meant to last

Fieldwork Goods was founded on a simple idea: the things you use every day should be beautiful, durable, and made by people who care. We partner with small workshops around the world to bring you goods that tell a story.

Every item in our catalog is reviewed, tested, and stocked in limited runs. No mass production. No corners cut. Just honest goods for people who appreciate the craft.
